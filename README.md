One line, 1 to 240 characters, that gives the judge a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a scene. A small program picks the line deterministically from hand-written candidates with a rubric drawn from the published judge brief — genuine earned pride, a concrete scene with a real person, pride earned inside the line and never asserted, landing in one read, and freshness — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

The winning line: an apprentice who burned every weld for a month finally lays one perfect bead, lifts his visor, and just stares at it, grinning.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
